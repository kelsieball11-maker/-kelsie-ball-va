Why I Do This Now

After spending nearly a decade in leadership and operations roles, I had a realization: the biggest bottleneck for most founders and business owners isn't strategy or talent—it's the stuff that doesn't have a clear owner. The processes nobody documents. The projects that live in someone's brain. The administrative work that eats up leadership time and slows everything down.

I left my corporate role because I got tired of watching smart people waste their energy on things that could be solved with better systems, clearer processes, and someone paying attention. I realized I'm happiest when I'm solving these problems—and I'm better at it than most people.

Now I work directly with founders and business owners who get it: that having someone thoughtful, detail-oriented, and resourceful handling the operational chaos is what frees them up to actually build something great.

How I Show Up

Every piece of work I do is guided by a set of values that shape how I partner with you.

Thoughtful

I care about the why, not just the what. I ask questions. I notice things other people miss.

Practical

Solutions that look good on paper but nobody uses aren't solutions. I build things people will actually want to use.

Direct

Clear communication beats unnecessary meetings. I'll tell you what I think, and I'll ask you to do the same.

Resourceful

Give me a problem and some context. I'll figure it out—or I'll find the person who can, and coordinate from there.

What You Can Expect

I'm not here to just take orders. I'm here to think with you. I'll notice when something isn't working. I'll probably find a better way to do something. And if I don't know how something works, I'll learn it. That's what you get when you work with someone who actually cares about making things work smoothly.
