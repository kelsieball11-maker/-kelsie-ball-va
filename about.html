About Kelsie

How I've spent the last decade building systems, streamlining operations, and learning that you don't have to choose between doing work that matters and being present for the people you love.

How we spend our time matters. For everyone, this looks different. For me, it has always been about doing work I love while honoring my role as a mom—being close to my kids, present for the daily moments, the volunteering, the sports, the vacations. That has meant finding work that doesn't force me to choose between career and family. It has meant loving what I do while keeping what matters most in focus.

The Journey

My career started in child care—literally. When my first child was born, I became director of a child care facility in Southern Utah. I wanted to be close to my son. But I also became obsessed with the operations, the processes, the efficiency. How could we serve kids better? How could we streamline what wasn't working?

That curiosity got noticed. I became a Regional Director supporting other centers in the area. I streamlined operations, built curriculum templates, created training programs that gave every center structure and consistency. Then I moved into a broader role—Director of Operations for the entire company across six facilities and the corporate office. I owned documentation, grants, classroom standards, food programs, and compliance. I learned to see the whole system and make it work better.

When my kids got a bit older, I was ready to stretch into new spaces. I took an Executive Assistant role at WGU—supporting executives in product and portfolio management. I managed calendars, coordinated day-to-day activities, planned events, and handled expenses. But again, the operational side called me. I took on training and onboarding, which led to a Technical Portfolio Operations role where I worked on standards, finances, onboarding, headcount management, vendor relationships, and even built automated AI solutions to streamline technology standards across the entire organization.

It was incredible work. Strategic, high-impact, using every skill I'd honed over the years. But then the company's priorities shifted. Full return to office was on the horizon, and that didn't align with my core belief. I realized I'd spent years building someone else's vision of success, and I was ready to build my own.

Why This, Why Now

I have skills. Real, hard-won skills in operations, systems, automation, documentation, executive support, project management, and strategy. Skills that help businesses move faster and leaders focus on what matters.

But I also have a life I want to live. I have kids I want to be present for. A belief that you can do incredible work and be there for the moments that matter. You don't have to choose.

So I'm here to work with founders and leaders who get it. Who believe that efficiency isn't about grinding harder—it's about working smarter. Who want someone in their corner who will think strategically about their business, own the details, and actually care about moving things forward. Someone who knows that business is about people. Always.
