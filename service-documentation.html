SOPs & Process Documentation

Getting knowledge out of heads and into systems so your business doesn't depend on one person.

What This Looks Like

Right now, someone on your team (probably you) is the person who knows how to do everything. Processes exist in conversations, emails, notes, or just muscle memory. When you onboard new people, you spend weeks explaining things. If someone takes time off, work slows down.

SOPs turn that scattered knowledge into documented, repeatable processes. Not 200-page manuals. Clear, practical guides that let people get things done without asking you questions. It's how you scale without staying as the bottleneck.

Typically Includes

Here's what SOP documentation usually covers. (But this is flexible—we customize based on what actually needs to move.)

Process Audits
Identifying which processes actually need documentation and where the gaps are.
SOP Creation & Writing
Documenting processes clearly and concisely so anyone can follow them.
Documentation Templates
Creating templates so your team can document new processes consistently going forward.
Knowledge Transfer
Getting the knowledge from your head (or your team's heads) into written form.
Training Materials
Creating resources for onboarding and ongoing training based on your processes.
Process Refinement
Updating and improving SOPs as your business evolves.
The customization piece: Some businesses need a few critical processes documented. Others need a whole playbook. We start with what's actually holding you back and build from there.

This Is Customized, Not Unlimited

I don't believe in creating documentation for documentation's sake. During our intake call, we identify the processes that are costing you the most—things that keep you bottlenecked, onboarding challenges, knowledge that needs to be captured. Then we document strategically around those priorities.

You get processes that actually scale your business, not a filing cabinet of unused manuals.
