Executive & Founder Support

The details, coordination, and follow-through that keep leaders focused on what actually matters. You handle strategy. I handle everything else.

What This Looks Like

When you're running something, there are a thousand moving pieces. Calendars that need management. Meetings that need prep. Travel logistics. Communication flows. People waiting on answers. Projects that need someone to own the details.

This is where I come in. I become the person who knows what's happening, what needs to happen next, and who needs to be involved. I keep commitments moving. I catch the things that slip through. I'm the reliable person behind the scenes so you can stay focused on the work that only you can do.

Typically Includes

Here's what executive support often covers. (But this is flexible—we customize based on what actually needs to move.)

Calendar & Meeting Management
Scheduling, blocking time for focused work, meeting prep and follow-up.
Travel & Logistics
Booking, coordination, expense tracking, itinerary management.
Communication Management
Email triage, drafting, managing important conversations.
Priority & Task Management
Tracking what matters, managing competing priorities, moving things forward.
Meeting Preparation
Agenda building, stakeholder prep, follow-up documentation.
Executive Communication
Helping translate vision, managing key relationships, stakeholder coordination.
The customization piece: Not every founder needs all of this. Some need heavy calendar management. Others need someone owning their inbox or coordinating specific projects. During our intake call, we talk through what's actually taking up your mental space and build from there.

This Is Customized, Not Unlimited

I don't believe in one-size-fits-all support. During our intake call, we map out your actual priorities—what's taking up your mental space, what's the real bottleneck, where does focus matter most? Then we build a package that addresses those specific things.

You get someone thinking strategically about your work, not just checking boxes.
