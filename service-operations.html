Business Operations & Organization

Turning scattered workflows and competing priorities into practical structure that actually works for your team.

What This Looks Like

Most growing businesses have the same problem: systems that worked when you were small don't scale. Workflows are inconsistent. People don't know who owns what. Information lives in different places. Communication breaks down.

This is where operations work comes in. I map out how things actually work right now, identify the friction points, and design systems that make work clearer for everyone. Not complicated systems—practical ones that fit how your team actually operates.

Typically Includes

Here's what operations organization usually covers. (But this is flexible—we customize based on what actually needs to move.)

Process Mapping & Audit
Understanding how things currently work, identifying bottlenecks and inefficiencies.
Workflow Design
Creating or restructuring workflows that make sense for your team and scale.
Tool & System Setup
Selecting, implementing, and configuring systems that work together.
Accountability & Ownership
Making it clear who owns what, when things are due, and how work moves.
Team Training & Alignment
Getting everyone on the same page about how to work together.
Ongoing Optimization
Monitoring what's working, adjusting what isn't, staying ahead of growth.
The customization piece: Not every business needs all of this. Some need help redesigning one critical workflow. Others need a full operational overhaul. We figure out what's actually costing you time and money, then we fix that.

This Is Customized, Not Unlimited

I don't believe in one-size-fits-all operations consulting. During our intake call, we map out your actual priorities—what's creating the most friction, where's the biggest breakdown, what's keeping your team from being effective? Then we build a plan that addresses those specific things.

You get someone thinking strategically about your work, not just checking boxes.
