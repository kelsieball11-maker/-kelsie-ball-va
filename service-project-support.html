Project & Implementation Support

The person who owns getting things done—from planning to execution to completion.

What This Looks Like

You have important initiatives. Major projects. Things that need to happen. But they stall. People get busy with day-to-day work. Nobody owns it. Communication breaks down. And six months later, it still isn't done.

This is where project and implementation support comes in. I become the person who owns the timeline, tracks progress, coordinates the moving pieces, removes roadblocks, and makes sure things actually get finished. Not project management software—a real person making sure your priorities move.

Typically Includes

Here's what project support usually covers. (But this is flexible—we customize based on what actually needs to move.)

Project Planning & Strategy
Breaking down what needs to happen, creating realistic timelines, identifying dependencies.
Stakeholder Coordination
Making sure everyone knows their role, deadlines are clear, communication flows.
Progress Tracking
Monitoring status, flagging delays early, keeping things on schedule.
Roadblock Resolution
Identifying what's slowing things down and getting things unstuck quickly.
Initiative Ownership
Being the person people know is responsible for getting this done.
Execution to Completion
Staying with it until it's actually done, not just planned.
The customization piece: Some projects need light support. Others need deep involvement. We figure out the right level of ownership based on what you're trying to accomplish and what your team needs.

This Is Customized, Not Unlimited

I don't take on every project. During our intake call, we talk through what you're trying to accomplish, why it matters, what's been blocking it, and what success looks like. Then we decide together if this is a fit and what support actually looks like.

You get someone who's genuinely invested in seeing it through, not just managing tasks.
